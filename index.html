Proudly announced to the T1D #WeAreNotWaiting community at D‑Data 2026…

Omnipod 5 is coming to Loop & Trio this summer

How you can prepare Read the FAQ

What’s happening?

We’ve gotten Omnipod 5 pods working with Loop and Trio! We’re still testing it carefully, and it should be ready for more people to use this summer.

Loop and Trio are apps that run on an iPhone and help people with type 1 diabetes. They connect to your glucose sensor and insulin pump and adjust your insulin automatically, so you have to think about it less. They’re built and improved by a community of volunteers, with support from the Nightscout Foundation.

How you can prepare

We built new software (called OmnipodKit) that lets these apps work with every kind of Omnipod — including the Omnipod 5. It’s already in the newest version of Trio and in the test version of Loop, but the Omnipod 5 part isn’t turned on yet. You can get set up now (and help us test) using the Omnipods you already have:

  1. Update to the latest version of Loop or Trio.
  2. After your current pod runs out, remove your current pump from the app.
  3. Choose “All Omnipod Types” from the pump menu (this is the new software).
  4. Turn on the Data Sharing option, then start using OmnipodKit with the Omnipods you have now.

Frequently asked questions

Can I update my open-source AID app right now to switch to Omnipod 5 pods?

We’re excited too! OmnipodKit is already available in the latest version of Trio and the dev branch of Loop. Our developers have put multiple years of work into a unified pump driver that works with Eros, DASH, and Omnipod 5 pods.

However, the ability to use Omnipod 5 is still being tested and won’t show up yet. You can help us by enabling the Data Sharing option and using the OmnipodKit option with your current DASH or Eros pods.

I have an Android phone. Can I put Loop or Trio on it and use Omnipod 5 pods that way?

No. Loop and Trio are for iOS (iPhone) only. You can get an iPhone and switch to OS-AID, or wait for the AndroidAPS devs to make AndroidAPS work with Omnipod 5 pods.

Keep up to date on their progress by monitoring the AAPS Users Facebook group or the WeAreNotWaiting Discord server. Tentatively, the AndroidAPS developers hope to support Omnipod 5 by the end of the year — however, there is still much additional work needed.

Does the iPhone 16 or 17e still have connection issues with Omnipod 5 pods?

There has been no indication that the iPhone 16 or 17e have the same connection issues with Omnipod 5 pods as with DASH Atlas pods.

Does the Omnipod 5 have the 203 errors?

We can’t guarantee you won’t encounter a 203 error or any other error, but at this time there has been no indication that the frequency of 203 errors persists with Omnipod 5 pods.

When will Omnipod 5 be released so I can use it?

When it’s ready™️

You can already use the new Omnipod driver, OmnipodKit. The Omnipod 5 portion is still in closed-beta testing, but you can use the new OmnipodKit by updating to the latest version of Loop/Trio, deleting your pump after your pod dies, and selecting “All Omnipod Types” from the pump menu.

Can I be a closed beta tester? Do you need more testers?

At this time, a select number of developers and experienced users are doing highly involved testing. Once it is stable enough for advanced users, it will be released to the dev branch of Trio/Loop.

At that time, if you are willing and able to report potential bugs and issues, we welcome your contribution to testing so it can reach the main branch more quickly, ensuring all users have access to this advancement.

Does this mean the Tandem Mobi is also close?

The same developers working on Omnipod 5 are also working on Mobi. Omnipod 5 took priority due to the drastic increase in 203 errors and iPhone 16/17e communication issues. We are still working on Tandem Mobi and hope to have an update for you… soon™️…
